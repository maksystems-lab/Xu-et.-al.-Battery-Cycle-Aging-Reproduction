## 1. Research problem
    Cycle aging of battery cost is a substantial chunk of the total costs. So as a battery is charged and discharged several times in a day or in a period of interval of time,  and gradually in its life, it tends to degrade materially and hence that requires hefty revenues to be replensihed/ reinstalled/ renewed. Hence, whilst the bidding of electricity and revenue building and electricity arbitrage is carried out, this battery cycle aging/ degradation , a factor, be rigorously rendered consequential. 

    Economics comes into the game because the cycle aging renewal costs tend to shoot >>> the electricity bidding and selling revenue generation itself.. hence.. the minimum most non profitable outcome is to atleast match the electricity cost with the battery battery degradation cosst, so the proposed solution is breaking down the problem and seeking to define the marginal cost of aging cycle of battery of each CYCLE. So then we would find it facilitated to find out the number of cycles per day / per hour or something to actually see how much profits from delivering the electricity we made verses the cost accumulating on the cycle aging of the battery degradation itsef. 

    The paper proposes a linear piece wise cost function  which it says is a close approximation of the cycle aging costs of batteries. this model can be incorporated into the market and will eliminate the jargon of calculations and will facilitate whether how effieicntly bess shud participate in the wholesale markets.. giving insights into operation cost. , because per cycle aging cost is the first principle of the model, the bes owners cann so design market offers such that they can atleast tend to recover cost of battery life lost to market dispatch.
